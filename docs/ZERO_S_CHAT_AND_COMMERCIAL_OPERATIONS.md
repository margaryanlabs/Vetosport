# Project ZERO-S — chat operating protocol & ethical commercial launch
Date: 2026-10-11

## Reality gate
- ZERO-S has no observed positive out-of-sample betting edge, no 2026 matched odds series, and no executable pricing/limits.
- Betting micro-odds at 1.05 is not a salary substitute. A $200 loss absorbs 20 $10 wins.
- The product cannot claim autonomous ChatGPT without a real paid server-side inference integration. The **copy-case** handoff in Lab 001 requires human action and clearly says so.
- Browser-only paper journal records are not cryptographically immutable and do not prove returns.
- Starting with conditional research rather than gambling execution protects the project from claiming non-existent results.

## Chat operating procedure — one match at a time
1. Enter team/competition, score, game minute, half-goal Under line, displayed decimal quote and its source into the ZERO-S Late Tail research room.
2. Press **Copy Case for ChatGPT**, paste into the conversation. Do not present invented demo parameters as evidence.
3. In chat, request five outputs: (a) exact contract semantics, (b) price break-even, (c) scenario sensitivity incl. extra time, (d) freshness / suspended market / model calibration gaps, (e) research-only verdict.
4. Enter the paper scenario in Lab 003 **before** the game outcome. Record ALL evaluated situations, even rejected ones.
5. Later fill observed outcomes manually, export CSV, label as user-reported. For independent proof, cross-check event IDs and provider timestamps and run a deterministic settlement worker on the server.
6. Do not add a live-feed claim, win-rate pitch, or automatic wagering until source and empirical validation are established.

## Independent research milestones
- H1: test season shift of late goals using 2023–24 or newer event data with exact added minutes.
- H2: falsify "recent goal implies 2+ more goals" in future independent samples; control match state and minute.
- H3: compare goal hazard (Poisson/score-aware/time-aware/self-exciting) with proper leave-season-forward holdout.
- H4: compare model probabilities against *real contemporaneously executable* prices net of bookmaker margin, with quote suspension/latency.
- P0: no model promotion if accuracy, reliability, and freshness fail. PASS is always a valid outcome.

## Potential commercial customer, distinct from betting
Focus on **sports publishers, content studios, football analytics consultancies and independent teams**:
- Research/visual report pilot: €100–€300 project *test pricing*, not demand proof.
- Interactive match-analysis micro-dashboard: €500–€1,500 setup *test pricing*, based on scope and provider rights.
- Monthly audience-facing match research summary: €100–€300/mo *test pricing*, with explicit research disclaimers.
These are not forecasts of sales, employment or recurring income. First validate willingness to pay through direct online conversations and demo review. Use genuine historical statistics and state what data is unavailable.

## First paid-pilot experiment
1. Publish one verified VETO Sport demo and a one-page sample from ZERO-S.
2. Independently identify 20 potential B2B customers; select those buying sports-data visualization, editorial analytics or data-storytelling, not "guaranteed tips".
3. Offer one **bounded analysis deliverable** with 72-hour fixed requirements and explicit data rights.
4. Count responses, discovery calls, written paid pilot acceptances and delivery costs; report observed, not projected, conversion.
5. If no traction, iterate the offer; do not compensate by chasing losses from sports betting.

## Georgia legal and data compliance
If promoting or distributing sports-betting offerings in Georgia, obtain a local legal review first; gambling advertising has substantial restrictions under the Law of Georgia on Advertising. Sports analytics is a different business model, but avoid marketing that promotes gambling implicitly. Source: https://www.matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/31840?publication=33 . Do not redistribute proprietary bookmaker feeds or StatsBomb open events outside licensed terms.

## Research product promise
Market the technology as **decision transparency, outcome auditability and uncertainty quantification**. Never as guaranteed micro-profit or automatic gambling execution.
